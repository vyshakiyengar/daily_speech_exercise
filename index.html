Voice On

What this is

A short daily practice for speech fluency. It runs itself: press start and it walks you through, one screen at a time, timing each step and moving on by itself.

  1. Do it out loud. Every step is spoken. Somewhere you can use your full voice.
  2. Same sequence every day. Only the reading material changes.
  3. Eight minutes by default. Switch to the longer version if any of it feels unclear — it teaches each piece on its own first.
  4. Turn on spoken guidance below if you would rather listen than read the screen.
Each step shows a count and a timer. The count is the target; the timer is how long you have. Finish early, start again until the timer runs out.
Your own name is the single most commonly feared word for people who stutter, so the drills use it. Stored on this device only.
