2. What Speech On is, and what it is not

The Service offers guided exercises in speaking aloud: moving the lips and tongue, breathing, pacing, pausing and reading passages. It is intended solely as a general wellbeing and practice activity for people who want to warm up or practise speaking.

Speech On is not a medical device. It is not intended for the diagnosis, prevention, monitoring, prediction, prognosis, treatment or alleviation of any disease, injury or disability. It makes no diagnostic or therapeutic claim. Nothing in the Service should be treated as medical advice or as a clinical intervention.

Specifically, the Service is not a treatment for stuttering, dysarthria, apraxia of speech, aphasia, voice disorders, articulation or phonological disorders, or any other diagnosed condition. It does not assess your speech, does not measure it, and cannot tell you whether anything is wrong. The exercises have not been clinically validated as a programme.

If you have a speech, language, voice or swallowing concern, please consult a qualified speech and language professional. The Service is not a substitute for that, and using it is not a reason to delay seeking help.
